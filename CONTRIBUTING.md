# Contributing

Super Sparrow does not accept contributions from outside the team. Pull requests, patches, and code suggestions from people who are not authorized members of the Anthillock team will be closed without review.

This keeps the ownership of the code clear under the [license](LICENSE.md).

Authorized team members follow [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) and the plan in [docs/PLAN.md](docs/PLAN.md).

If you found a security problem, email KR Shanto at the address in the license instead of opening a public issue.
